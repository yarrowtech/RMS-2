# RMS Vendor Business & Growth Platform

## A simpler way for vendors and retailers to work together

RMS brings vendor discovery, product catalogues, quotations, purchase orders, delivery updates, invoices, payments, returns and business insights into one connected platform. Instead of managing orders through scattered WhatsApp messages, calls, spreadsheets and paper records, vendors and retailers can work from the same verified business flow.

## Who can use RMS?

RMS can support different types of vendor businesses, including:

- Manufacturers
- Wholesalers and distributors
- General suppliers
- Fabric and raw-material suppliers
- Finished-goods vendors
- Exporters and importers
- Packaging suppliers
- Job-work and production partners
- Logistics and service providers
- Small businesses, regional brands and growing enterprises
- Sales agents and authorized business representatives

One vendor business can maintain a single RMS identity while working with multiple approved retailers. Each retailer relationship remains separate, secure and controlled.

## Why should a vendor use RMS?

- Present products through a structured digital catalogue.
- Receive retailer enquiries and RFQs in one place.
- Submit quotations with clear prices, quantities and commercial terms.
- Negotiate without losing important information in calls or chat history.
- Receive and respond to purchase orders digitally.
- Update production, dispatch and expected-delivery information.
- View invoices, payments, outstanding amounts and disputes.
- Build an organized business history with each retailer.
- Discover new business connections through the RMS Business Network.
- Reduce manual follow-ups and repeated data entry.
- Give teams one reliable source for order and payment information.

## Vendor growth opportunities

RMS helps a vendor become more visible, responsive and operationally ready.

- **Better discovery:** Approved retailers can find relevant vendors and catalogue products.
- **Faster response:** Centralized RFQs, messages and order information reduce delays.
- **More professional selling:** Structured catalogues and quotations build buyer confidence.
- **Repeat business:** Accurate order history makes reordering and relationship management easier.
- **Wider network:** Vendors can connect with retailers and other businesses through the Business Network.
- **Stronger planning:** Order, invoice and outstanding-payment visibility helps vendors plan production and cash flow.
- **Scalable operations:** A vendor can manage more retailer relationships without depending entirely on spreadsheets and personal chat records.

RMS supports growth, but it does not guarantee sales, retailer approval, orders, profit or payment. Business results depend on product quality, pricing, capacity, service, compliance and retailer demand.

## How RMS unifies vendor management, retail operations and POS

RMS connects the commercial journey instead of treating each activity as a separate system.

```text
Vendor catalogue
      ↓
Retailer enquiry / RFQ
      ↓
Quotation and negotiation
      ↓
Purchase order
      ↓
Vendor production and dispatch
      ↓
Retailer goods receipt and stock update
      ↓
Inventory available to stores and POS
      ↓
Retail sale and stock reduction
      ↓
Invoice, payment and business reporting
```

### Vendor point of view

The vendor receives clearer demand, confirmed orders, delivery expectations, invoice information and payment visibility.

### Retailer point of view

The retailer gets controlled vendor onboarding, purchasing, receiving, inventory, finance and store-level visibility.

### POS point of view

Products received and accepted into retailer inventory can become available for store operations and billing. POS sales then reduce stock and contribute to sales and inventory reporting. Actual stock movement depends on the retailer completing the required receipt and inventory steps; a vendor dispatch update alone does not add stock.

## Rate of interest and finance clarification

RMS is primarily a business operations and vendor-retailer collaboration platform. RMS does not automatically provide a loan, credit facility or guaranteed early-payment finance.

- There is no standard "rate of interest" unless a separate financing product is formally offered.
- Any credit period between a retailer and vendor must be agreed in their purchase order or contract.
- Any interest, late-payment charge or discount must be clearly agreed by the parties and comply with applicable law.
- If financing is offered through a bank, NBFC or another regulated partner, the partner must disclose the interest rate, processing fees, annualized cost, repayment schedule, penalties and eligibility conditions.
- RMS should never advertise "zero interest," "guaranteed credit" or a fixed finance rate without an approved written financing arrangement.

Suggested public wording:

> Payment terms and credit periods are agreed between the vendor and retailer. Any financing facility, if introduced, will be subject to the financing partner's eligibility criteria, interest rate, fees and separate terms.

## Limitations and considerations

- Retailer approval is not guaranteed.
- RMS does not guarantee enquiries, orders, revenue, profit or payment.
- Vendors must keep catalogue, price, availability and tax information accurate.
- Internet access and user training are required.
- Retailers and vendors must still follow applicable tax, product, labour, privacy and commercial laws.
- Some features may depend on subscription plan, configuration or third-party services.
- WhatsApp, payment gateways, logistics and financing require separate provider setup and approval.
- Incorrect data or incomplete operational steps can affect inventory and reports.
- Commercial disputes remain the responsibility of the participating businesses unless a separate RMS service states otherwise.
