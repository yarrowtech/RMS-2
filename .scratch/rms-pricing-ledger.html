Estimate

WhatsApp is a written, unwired skeleton today — whatsapp_routes.py isn't even registered in main.py, and has no Meta Business Account, phone number, or verify token configured. Email already works over plain SMTP (email_utils.py) — whatever provider gets configured in the environment, at that provider's own rate, passed through rather than marked up here.

Automation & scheduled jobs

Live in system

Five real cron-style jobs exist today, each gated by a shared X-Cron-Secret header — but the backend process itself has no in-process scheduler, so every one of them sits idle until something external (a cloud scheduler, a cron VM) actually calls it daily. Methodology

  1. Live figures are read directly from the codebase's pricing constants — retailer plan tiers, admin-seat and store-slot add-ons, and vendor subscription tiers — not adjusted or rounded.
  2. Estimate figures are proposed pricing for capabilities that are either partially built (forecasting) or not yet built (fraud scoring, GenAI, per-seat billing, metered API/cloud) — anchored to the live figures' scale, not invented independently.
  3. AMC percentages and per-seat estimates follow common Indian B2B SaaS conventions (10–20% AMC, seat pricing 1–4× the platform's own per-unit add-on cost) rather than being pulled from any RMS billing logic.
  4. All figures exclude GST. Recompute before using in a customer-facing quote.
